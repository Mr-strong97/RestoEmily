# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

*Inferred from the existing client flows:* restaurant guests seated at Emily Hôtel & Restaurant who want to browse the menu and place an order quickly from their phone.

## Product Purpose

*Inferred from the existing routes and copy:* let guests access the restaurant menu, add items to a basket, submit an order, and follow that order from their table.

## Positioning

*Inferred from the QR-table flow:* a table-aware ordering experience that opens directly from the QR code, without an app or account.

## Operating Context

Guests use a personal mobile device while at a restaurant table. The QR code identifies the table; the service staff receives and prepares the resulting order.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed in the application: menu browsing, product details, basket, order tracking, table/QR access, restaurant information, and services. The home page reads restaurant details and optional services from the existing API. It must preserve the French language and the official Emily logo assets.

## Brand Commitments

Confirmed: Emily Hôtel & Restaurant; existing logo assets at `frontend/public/emily-logo.png` and `frontend/public/emily-logo-icon.png`. Visual direction is intentionally open; the user requested a professional landing page.

## Evidence on Hand

Real assets: the two supplied logo PNG files. No verified food photography, testimonials, awards, or operational claims were supplied; future surfaces must not invent them.

## Product Principles

* Make the first action obvious: access the menu.
* Keep ordering friction low on a phone at the table.
* Explain the QR-to-order flow plainly.
* Present live restaurant information only when it is available.
